# Privacy policy

Year 12,000 does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What the extension does

It reads the text of the pages you visit, inside your browser, to find dates and show their years in the Human Era (2026 becomes 12026). This happens entirely on your device. Nothing about the pages you visit, their content or your activity ever leaves your browser.

## What it stores

One setting: whether the extension is switched on or off. It is kept in your browser's local extension storage, on your device, and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## What it does not do

- It makes no network requests.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
- It loads no remote code.
- It does not read or change what you type into forms.

## Permissions

- **storage**: to remember the on/off switch.
- **Access to all sites**: dates can appear on any page, so the extension has to run on any page to convert them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this file, and its history is public in this repository.
